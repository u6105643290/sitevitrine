⌁ FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. That's the whole point. You describe the game in plain language; Bloxforge handles terrain, scripts, UI and publishing. If you ever want to peek at the generated Luau, it's there — but you never have to.

Do I need Roblox Studio installed?

No. Bloxforge is a standalone desktop app with its own live preview. Advanced creators can still open any generated game in Studio to fine-tune it by hand.

Who owns the games I create?

You do — entirely. Games publish to your own Roblox account, under your name. Visits, favorites and any monetization belong to you.

Can it change a game after publishing?

Yes. Reopen the project, describe the change — “add a winter event”, “double the map” — and push an update. Every version is kept, so you can roll back anytime.

How much does it cost?

Bloxforge is free to download and free for your first games. Bigger worlds and faster forging come with Pro. No card required to start.

Is my Roblox account safe?

Bloxforge only ever publishes — and only when you click Publish. It never plays, trades, chats or spends on your behalf, and you can disconnect it at any time.
